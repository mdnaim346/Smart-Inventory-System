Smart Inventory

Know what to buy, how much, from whom and when, and why.

What it does

Demand forecasting

  • Weighted 7/30/90-day demand with a day-of-week pattern, per product and warehouse
  • Forecast history and accuracy (Mean Absolute Percentage Error) per product
  • Optional Claude AI: adjusts the forecast within a limit you set, and writes a one-line risk insight
  • Daily AI call budget; without AI the statistical forecast keeps working

Smart replenishment

  • Reorder point and safety stock from vendor lead time and service level (by ABC class)
  • Order quantity for your target days of cover, vendor minimum quantity and pack size
  • Cheapest valid vendor, or your preferred vendor
  • One draft purchase order per vendor and warehouse, no duplicates
  • Transfer from a warehouse with excess stock before buying
  • Large restock orders wait for a manager's approval

Stock health

  • Low stock on live usable quantity (expired lots excluded)
  • Dead stock detection; dead products are never restocked
  • Lots expiring soon
  • Monthly ABC (value) and XYZ (demand variability) classes

Dashboard and reports

  • Stock value, turnover, days of cover, fill rate and stockouts
  • Filters by period, category, warehouse, vendor and risk
  • Forecast chart per product: sales, forecast and stock
  • XLSX and PDF exports, daily risk e-mail digest

Sales automation (each one optional)

  • Block out-of-stock sales
  • Validate deliveries automatically, with or without backorders
  • Post invoices automatically

Setup

  1. Install the module. Smart Inventory Managers get the Settings page under Smart Inventory > Configuration.
  2. Set the forecast horizon, service levels, target days of cover and approval amount.
  3. Optional AI: run pip install anthropic on the server, turn on Use AI Provider and enter your Claude API key.
  4. Open the Dashboard and press Run Restock, or let the hourly job do it.

Two access groups: User (see dashboard and forecasts) and Manager (settings, restock, approvals).
